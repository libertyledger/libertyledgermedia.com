Surveillance Without Consent: What Texarkana's Flock Safety Records Show

Since June 2023, eleven cameras have been tracking every vehicle moving through key parts of Texarkana — under a contract the city council never voted on. This investigation, based entirely on documents obtained under the Texas Public Information Act, examines the contract, the data-sharing network, and the gaps in oversight. City Manager David Orr and Council Member Jay Davis were given the opportunity to respond before publication; both responses are published in full.
